01 — DBMS

Database Management Systems

The subject most likely to show up disguised as an HLD deep dive ("why did you pick this index?") rather than as its own round.

•Normalization: 1NF → BCNF — and, just as important, when to deliberately denormalize for read performance.
•Indexing: B-tree vs hash index, clustered vs non-clustered, composite index column order, covering indexes — and why an index can make writes slower.
•Transactions: ACID properties, isolation levels (Read Uncommitted → Serializable) and which anomalies each one prevents (dirty read, non-repeatable read, phantom read).
•Concurrency control: optimistic vs pessimistic locking, MVCC (how Postgres/MySQL InnoDB actually implement it).
•Joins & query execution: nested loop vs hash vs merge join, and reading an EXPLAIN plan.
•Storage engines: B-tree (Postgres/InnoDB) vs LSM-tree (Cassandra/RocksDB) — write-amplification vs read-amplification trade-offs, covered in more depth on the HLD page.
