If your loop includes Spring Boot, it's rarely asked in isolation — Apple folds it into the same 60-minute slot as a DSA problem, and Uber wants resilience patterns (circuit breakers, rate limiting) explained and implemented, not just defined. This page covers core Boot mechanics, microservices resilience, persistence, and production topics, with a dedicated Resilience4j resource set since that's the question both companies keep asking.
