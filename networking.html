Protocols & the request lifecycle

"What happens when you type a URL and hit enter" is still asked directly — and it's also the backbone of every networking building block on the HLD page.

•OSI / TCP-IP model: know the layers well enough to place any protocol you're asked about correctly, on the spot.
•TCP vs UDP: three-way handshake, reliability/ordering guarantees, and when UDP's lack of guarantees is the right trade-off (video, gaming, DNS).
•HTTP evolution: HTTP/1.1 vs HTTP/2 (multiplexing, header compression) vs HTTP/3 (QUIC over UDP) — and why each version exists.
•TLS handshake: what a certificate actually proves, and roughly how the handshake establishes a shared key.
•DNS resolution: the full chain — browser cache → OS cache → recursive resolver → root → TLD → authoritative server.
•The full "type a URL" flow: DNS resolution → TCP handshake → TLS handshake (if HTTPS) → HTTP request/response → render — be ready to narrate all five steps without prompting.
