16-week Big Tech roadmap

This is the longer track — a phased plan across five tracks (DSA, LLD, HLD, CS fundamentals, behavioral) for candidates targeting multiple companies at once — Microsoft (L61/62), Uber (L4/L5), Amazon (SDE-2/L5), Google (L4), Meta (E4), and Apple (ICT3) — rather than a single fast-tracked loop. Built around a calibration of existing strengths and five specific gaps to close, at ~18–20 hrs/week, with applications staggered from Week 9 so you're not interviewing before you're ready.

Closing priorities

DSA skill alone won't secure an SDE-2 offer. Verbalizing reasoning out loud is critical to interview scoring. Production system experience is the key differentiator against typical competing candidates — plan to walk through it fluently in every HLD and behavioral round.
